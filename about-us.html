Why We Built Tradez Log

Most traders spend hours searching for strategies, indicators, and signals — but very few actually track their performance deeply enough to improve. That’s the gap we wanted to solve.

We noticed that many traders:

  • Repeat the same mistakes
  • Trade emotionally
  • Lack proper review systems
  • Depend only on memory
  • Never truly understand what works for them

Without data, improvement becomes guesswork. Tradez Log helps traders move from random trading to structured decision-making, from emotional reactions to data-driven confidence, and from inconsistency to continuous improvement.
